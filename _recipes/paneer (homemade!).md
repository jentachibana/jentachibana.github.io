## Ingredients

- 2060g milk
- 35g vinegar
- cheese cloth 

## Instructions

1. gently boil and stir milk and vinegar on stove top till liquid becomes green and milk curds float on top

2. filter out curds in cheese cloth and wrap tightly (squeeze out liquid)

3. let it set for 3-4 hours

4. cut into cubes
