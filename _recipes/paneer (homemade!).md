## Ingredients

- milk
- vinegar
- cheese cloth 

## Instructions

1. heat milk and vinegar on stove top till it becomes green and milk curds float on top

2. filter out curds in cheese cloth and wrap tightly (squeeze out liquid)

3. let it set for a couple of hours

4. cut into cubes
