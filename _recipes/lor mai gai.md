## Ingredients

- 30ml neutral oil 
- 6g dried shrimp 
- 3-5 dried shiitake mushrooms
- 1 chinese sausage 
- 1 green onion/scallion
- 570g daikon radish
- 235-355 ml water
- 155g rice flour
- 8g cornstarch
- salt
- sugar
- white pepper

## Instructions

1. wash, soak, and dice shitake mushroom, shrimp, green onion and chinese sausage
2. fry still brown
3. grate radish then add 2/3 of water and simmer for 10 min
4. mix rice flour and cornstarch with remaining 1/3 of water and the mushroom-sausage mix with sugar, salt and white pepper
5. mix into turnip cake and kill heat
6. oil dish and spread evenly inside
7. steam on medium-high for ~50 min
8. let set in fridge and freeze later
