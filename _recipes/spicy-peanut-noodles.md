## Ingredients


- 8 oz lo mein noodles
- 3 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 cucumber, julienned
- 1/4 cup crushed peanuts
- Fresh cilantro

## Instructions


1. Cook noodles according to package directions, drain and rinse with cold water.
2. Whisk together peanut butter, soy sauce, sriracha, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and garlic.
3. Toss noodles with the sauce until evenly coated.
4. Top with julienned cucumber, crushed peanuts, and cilantro.
5. Serve cold or at room temperature.
