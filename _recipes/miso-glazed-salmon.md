## Ingredients


- 4 salmon fillets
- 3 tbsp white miso paste
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 2 green onions, sliced

## Instructions


1. Whisk together miso paste, mirin, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and ginger.
2. Marinate salmon fillets for at least 30 minutes (up to overnight).
3. Preheat oven to 400°F.
4. Place salmon on a lined baking sheet and broil for 8-10 minutes until caramelized.
5. Garnish with sliced green onions and serve with rice.
