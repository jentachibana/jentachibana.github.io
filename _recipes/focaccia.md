## Ingredients

- 512g bread flour
- 8g dry active yeast
- 455g water
- 13g salt

## Instructions

1. activate yeast in warm water for ~10 min
2. mix flour and salt
3. add yeast and water to dry
4. let sit for 30 min
5. fold dough a couple of times with wet fingers
6. oil the dough
7. rest in fridge for a day
8. spread out on baking sheet and dent the dough
9. add rosemary, salt, and more olive oil
10. bake at 425 F for ~25 min
