## Ingredients

- 240g flour
- 150g cornmeal 
- 100g sugar
- 18g baking powder
- 6g salt
- 113g butter
- 110g oil
- 300g milk
- 3 eggs

## Instructions

1. wet + dry → oven (350 F for ~35 minutes)
