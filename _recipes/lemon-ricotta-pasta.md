## Ingredients


- 1 lb rigatoni
- 1 cup ricotta cheese
- Zest and juice of 2 lemons
- 1/2 cup parmesan, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- Fresh basil
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions


1. Cook rigatoni in salted water until al dente, reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
2. In a large pan, warm olive oil over medium heat and sauté garlic for 1 minute.
3. Remove from heat and stir in ricotta, lemon zest, lemon juice, and parmesan.
4. Add pasta and toss, adding pasta water a splash at a time until creamy.
5. Season with red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Top with fresh basil.
